AN OVERVIEW

Top Manufacturer of Plastic Dana & PP Granules

Jindal Polymers is a trusted name in the plastic processing industry, recognized as a leading Manufacturer and Supplier of high-grade plastic raw materials. Our product range includes Natural PP Granules, Milky PP Granules, Colored PP Granules, Colored PPCP Granules and more.

Our manufacturing unit is equipped with modern machinery and quality control systems, enabling us to produce granules and raw materials that meet diverse industrial needs. Every product is crafted in accordance with industry standards, and we maintain a wide inventory to fulfill both bulk and urgent orders efficiently.
